## 2023-10-27 - Contextual Empty States in TUIs
**Learning:** In TUI applications, empty states must be clearly differentiated to clarify whether the overall list is completely empty or just filtered by search. Furthermore, missing empty states should be replaced with helpful guidance and call-to-actions to improve usability.
**Action:** Always provide contextually differentiated empty state messaging and ensure the rendering layout adjusts (e.g. by setting numRendered) to prevent layout jumping in paginated views.
